Recommended workflow

  1. Add official Acts, Rules, notifications, circulars and judgments to the Source Library.
  2. Upload searchable PDFs or paste relevant extracts.
  3. Ask the Advisor a specific question and review its matching sources.
  4. Generate an audit from the verification framework.
  5. Record evidence, findings and corrective action.
  6. Export a backup regularly.

Source quality

Prefer the applicable Act or Code, Gazette notification, Rules, statutory scheme, official circular/order and binding judicial decision.

Distinguish binding law from administrative guidance, commentary and unverified extracts.

Do not treat a search-result snippet as the full legal position.
