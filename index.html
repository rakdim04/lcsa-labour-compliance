Labour Codes Workspace

The four Labour Codes are shown as compliance frameworks. Before relying on any provision, verify the applicable commencement notification, Central/State Rules, amendments, exemptions and sector-specific requirements.

Code on Wages, 2019

Wages, minimum wages, payment of wages, deductions, bonus and related compliance controls.

Industrial Relations Code, 2020

Industrial relations, trade unions, standing orders, disputes, strikes, retrenchment and closure-related controls.

Code on Social Security, 2020

EPF, ESI, gratuity, maternity benefit and other social-security compliance areas.

OSH & Working Conditions Code, 2020

Occupational safety, health, working conditions, working hours, welfare and contract labour controls.

POSH / Workplace Compliance

Internal Committee, policy, complaint handling, awareness and reporting controls.

About LCSA

LCSA – Labour Compliance & Strategy Advisor is intended to organise compliance requirements, audit evidence, legal issues, case references, judgments and management actions.

Always verify current statutory provisions, commencement notifications, rules, circulars, departmental orders and judicial developments before taking final action.
