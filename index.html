Settings & Help

Data storage

Records are stored in this browser using local storage. They are not automatically shared across devices or users. Export backups regularly. Do not store unnecessary sensitive employee personal data.

Coverage and verification

  • Official links are entry points; check the original notification, circular, order or judgment.
  • News items entered by the user are marked “Unverified” until reviewed.
  • Verify publication date, effective date, amendments, stays, appeals, supersession and jurisdiction.
  • For dependable scheduled aggregation across many websites, use a permitted RSS/API feed or a backend service. Never place a private API key in this public client-side app.
