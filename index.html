Four Labour Codes

The LCSA source structure treats the four Labour Codes as the primary central labour-law framework.

Code on Wages, 2019

Wages, minimum wages, payment of wages, bonus, equal remuneration and related compliance controls.

Industrial Relations Code, 2020

Industrial relations, standing orders, trade unions, disputes, retrenchment and related controls.

Code on Social Security, 2020

Social security, EPF, ESI, gratuity, maternity, unorganised, gig and platform worker framework.

OSH & Working Conditions Code, 2020

Occupational safety, health, working conditions, contract labour, inter-State migrant workers and related controls.

Source Status

The four Labour Codes were announced as implemented from 21 November 2025 by the Ministry of Labour & Employment. 3

Rules and implementation material can involve both Central and State rule-making because labour is a Concurrent List subject. The Ministry has described Central and State rule-making roles in its implementation material. 4
